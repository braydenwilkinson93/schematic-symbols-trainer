Know every rung cold

Tap a card to flip it. Work category by category — and remember the one rule every drawing follows: contacts are always drawn in their resting state, coil dead, nothing pressed.

Diagrams follow the common ANSI / NEMA ladder conventions used on U.S. electrician licensing exams: L1 on the left, L2 on the right, loads on the right of each rung, and every contact drawn at rest. Pack 2 of the Learned Cold series — the companion to the Electrical Schematic Symbols Trainer.
