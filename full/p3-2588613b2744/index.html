Foundations

Ohm's Law & Power

E = I × R  ·  I = E ÷ R  ·  R = E ÷ I
P = E × I  ·  VA (1φ) = E × I
P (3φ) = 1.732 × E × I × PF
kWh = kW × hours

Two 10 Ω resistors in parallel = 5 Ω. In series = 20 Ω. Parallel always lands below the smallest resistor.

Article 430

Motor Circuit Rules

  • Conductors & protection: use Table FLC (430.250 / 430.248). Overloads: use nameplate FLA (430.6).
  • Branch conductor: 125% × FLC (430.22)
  • Overload max: 125% of nameplate (SF ≥ 1.15 or rise ≤ 40°C), otherwise 115% (430.32)
  • Feeder conductor: 125% of largest FLC + sum of the rest (430.24)
  • Feeder protection max: largest branch device + sum of other FLCs (430.62)
  • Disconnect ampere rating: at least 115% × FLC (430.110)
  • A 480V system uses the 460V column; a 240V system uses 230V.

Article 310

Ampacity & Adjustment

  • Start in the 90°C column for THHN derating; the final answer can't beat the termination temperature column (usually 75°C).
  • Small conductors (240.4(D)): 14 AWG → 15A, 12 AWG → 20A, 10 AWG → 30A max breaker, no matter what the 90°C column says.
  • Continuous load: size conductors and breakers at 125%.
  • Parallel conductors: 1/0 AWG minimum, same length, material, and size.

Chapter 9 · 314.16 · 314.28

Fill Rules

  • Raceway fill (Ch. 9, Table 1): 1 conductor 53% · 2 conductors 31% · 3 or more 40%. A nipple 24 in. or shorter may fill to 60%.
  • Box fill volume per conductor: 14 AWG 2.00 in³ · 12 AWG 2.25 in³ · 10 AWG 2.50 in³.
  • Each device yoke = 2 allowances. All equipment grounds together = 1 allowance. Internal clamps = 1 allowance.
  • Pull boxes: straight pull = 8 × largest raceway; angle pull = 6 × largest + the other raceways in the row.

Voltage drop · Informational notes to 210.19 & 215.2

Voltage Drop

VD (1φ) = 2 × K × I × L ÷ CM
VD (3φ) = 1.732 × K × I × L ÷ CM
CM needed = 2 × K × I × L ÷ VD allowed
K: copper 12.9 · aluminum 21.2

L is the one-way distance. Target: 3% on a branch circuit, 5% total feeder + branch. Circular mils: 12 AWG 6,530 · 10 AWG 10,380 · 8 AWG 16,510 · 6 AWG 26,240 · 4 AWG 41,110.

Article 220

Load Calculation Factors

  • Dwelling general lighting: 3 VA per ft² (Table 220.12)
  • Small-appliance circuits: 1,500 VA each, minimum two; laundry circuit: 1,500 VA
  • Dwelling demand (Table 220.42): first 3,000 VA at 100%, remainder at 35%
  • Range (Table 220.55): one 12 kW range = 8 kW demand; add 5% for each kW above 12
  • Dryer: 5,000 VA minimum or nameplate, whichever is larger; 5 dryers demand at 85%
  • Noncoincident loads (220.60): count only the larger — heating or cooling, never both
Article 450

Transformers

I (1φ) = kVA × 1,000 ÷ V
I (3φ) = kVA × 1,000 ÷ (V × 1.732)

Primary-only protection where primary current is 9A or more: maximum 125% of primary current — and where that doesn't land on a standard size, the next higher standard size is permitted (Table 450.3(B), Note 1).

All values follow the 2023 NEC: Table 430.250 / 430.248 full-load currents, Table 430.52 percentages, Table 310.16 ampacities, Chapter 9 Tables 1, 4, and 5, 314.16 box fill, and Article 220 demand factors. Voltage-drop limits are the Code's informational-note recommendations (3% branch / 5% total).
