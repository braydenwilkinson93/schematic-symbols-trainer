250.50 – 250.53 · Electrodes

Electrode Field Rules

  • Every electrode present is used — one bonded system (250.50).
  • Metal water pipe must be supplemented (250.53(D)(2)); GEC lands within 5 ft of entry (250.68(C)(1)); jumper around meters (250.53(D)(1)).
  • Single rod: 25 Ω or less, or add a second (250.53(A)(2)). Electrodes spaced ≥ 6 ft.
  • Rod/pipe: ≥ 8 ft in contact with soil; rock → 45° angle or 30-in. trench.
  • Ufer: 20 ft of 4 AWG bare Cu or ½-in. rebar, in 2 in. of concrete.
  • Ground ring: 20 ft of 2 AWG bare Cu, buried ≥ 30 in.
  • Never electrodes: gas piping and aluminum (250.52(B)).
250.4 · The ideas under everything

Rules That Run the Pack

  • Earth is NOT the fault path (250.4(A)(5)): 120 V through a 25 Ω rod = 4.8 A — no trip. The low-impedance metal EGC path trips the breaker.
  • The neutral bonds to ground once — at the service (250.24(A)(5)) or an SDS source (250.30). Never downstream (250.142(B)).
  • An EGC carries fault current only. Normal current on a ground = objectionable current (250.6) = a bond in the wrong place.
  • Connections: listed means only — exothermic welds, listed clamps, machine screws engaging 2+ threads. Never solder-only, never sheet-metal screws (250.8).
  • No EGC in the box → GFCI replacement marked "No Equipment Ground" (406.4(D)(2)). Ungrounded systems get ground detectors (250.21(B)).
